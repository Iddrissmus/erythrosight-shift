# Reading log

Two to three papers a week. One note each, written the same day.

**Format for each note — keep it to roughly 200 words:**

1. **Problem** — what question was the paper actually trying to answer?
2. **Approach** — what did they do? Data, method, evaluation.
3. **Result** — what did they find, and how convincing is the evidence?
4. **What I'd question** — a limitation the paper admits, a design choice I'd have made
   differently, or something I didn't understand and need to come back to.

Point 4 is the one that matters. A summary anyone could write from the abstract is not
worth the time; the note earns its place when it records a judgement.

**How to read:** abstract, then figures, then conclusion. *Then* go back for the
introduction and methods. Do not read linearly and do not stop to understand everything —
finish the pass, then decide whether a second pass is worth it.
